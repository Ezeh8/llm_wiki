# RankedLM Travel Policy

This document defines travel approval and expense procedures for all
RankedLM employees. Effective date: January 1, 2026.

## Pre-Approval
All travel must be pre-approved by a direct manager before booking.
Bookings made without prior approval are not eligible for
reimbursement.

## Receipt Submission
Employees should submit receipts within 30 days of travel completion.
Receipts submitted after this window may be denied at the manager's
discretion.

## Booking Standards
Employees are expected to book economy-class travel and mid-tier
accommodations unless a documented business reason requires otherwise.
Any deviation must be approved in advance by a director.

For questions about this policy, contact the RankedLM Finance
Operations team.
