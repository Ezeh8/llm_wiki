# RankedLM Legal & Compliance Policy

This document combines contract review procedures and regulatory
compliance obligations for RankedLM. Effective date: January 1, 2026.

## Contract Review Thresholds
- Contracts under $10,000 total value: department manager approval only.
- Contracts $10,000–$100,000: department manager + Legal review required.
- Contracts over $100,000: department manager + Legal + Finance
  sign-off required, plus executive approval for multi-year commitments.

## Contract Review Timeline
Legal review must be completed within 5 business days of submission.
Contracts flagged as high-risk (unusual liability terms, non-standard
indemnification clauses, or international jurisdiction) require an
additional 5 business days for specialist review.

## Signature Authority
Only individuals listed on the current RankedLM Signature Authority
Matrix may execute contracts on behalf of the company.

## Data Retention Schedule
- Financial records (invoices, expense reports, tax filings): 7 years.
- Employee records (performance reviews, disciplinary actions): 7
  years post-employment.
- Customer contracts and correspondence: 6 years post-termination.
- Internal audit logs and access records: 3 years.

## Legal Hold Procedure
When litigation, investigation, or regulatory inquiry is reasonably
anticipated, the Compliance team issues a legal hold notice suspending
normal deletion schedules for the affected data category. Legal holds
override all standard retention periods until formally released.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected data breach, unauthorized access, or compliance
violation must be reported to the RankedLM Compliance team within 24
hours of discovery.

For questions about this policy, contact RankedLM Legal & Compliance.
