# RankedLM Finance & Expense Policy

This document defines financial approval limits, reimbursement rates,
and payment terms for all RankedLM departments. Effective date:
January 1, 2026. This document supersedes all prior finance policy
drafts.

## Approval Thresholds
All expense requests must be approved based on the following thresholds:
- Requests under $1,000: approved by direct manager only.
- Requests between $1,000 and $9,999: require director approval.
- Requests of $10,000 or more: require CFO approval and a purchase order.
- Any single expense over $25,000 requires a signed contract on file
  before payment is issued.

## Travel Per Diem Rates
- Domestic travel: $75 per day.
- International travel: $110 per day.
- Meals-only per diem (no lodging): $45 per day.

## Vendor Payment Terms
Standard vendor invoice payment terms are Net 30 from the invoice date.
Vendors enrolled in the early-payment program receive payment in 10
days with a 2% discount applied to the invoice total. Late payments
accrue interest at 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance, starting
31 days after the invoice date.

## Reimbursement Deadlines
Employees must submit expense reimbursement requests within 60 days of
the purchase date. Requests submitted after 90 days will not be
reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Budget Variance
Any department exceeding its quarterly budget by more than 8% must
file a variance report with the RankedLM Finance team within 5
business days of the quarter close.

For questions about this policy, contact the RankedLM Finance
Operations team.
