# RankedLM Operations Policy

This document defines standard operating procedures for day-to-day
RankedLM business operations. Effective date: January 1, 2026.

## Equipment Requests
New equipment requests (laptops, monitors, peripherals) under $1,000
are approved by direct manager. Requests $1,000 and above follow the
standard Finance approval thresholds defined in the RankedLM Finance
& Expense Policy.

## Vendor Onboarding
New operational vendors (software tools, contracted services) must be
reviewed by the Operations team before procurement. Vendors handling
sensitive data must also pass a Compliance review per the RankedLM
Legal & Compliance Policy before onboarding is finalized.

## Incident Escalation
Operational incidents (system outages, service disruptions) must be
reported to the on-call Operations lead immediately upon discovery.
Incidents affecting customer-facing systems for more than 30 minutes
require a written post-incident report within 3 business days.

## Remote Work Equipment
Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a secure,
functional home office setup. Equipment issues should be reported to
Operations within 1 business day of discovery to minimize downtime.

For questions about this policy, contact RankedLM Operations.
